Exactica Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026
Developer: Nazar Kokhan
Contact: nasic.ua.73@gmail.com

Exactica is a financial calculator for iPhone, iPad and Android. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

  • We do not collect any data. Exactica has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting service and no server of its own.
  • Everything you type or save stays on your device.
  • The only outside party involved is Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), and only when you buy or restore Exactica Pro. They handle that under their own privacy policies.

What stays on your device

Exactica stores the following only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • the calculator's memory (stack, registers, modes, settings for cash flows and loans);
  • the tape (history of your calculations);
  • saved programs;
  • your settings, including custom themes you create;
  • a small local error log (time and technical details of an internal error, with no numbers you entered), used only on the device;
  • the trial record (see below).

We cannot see any of this. It is never sent to us.

The 72-hour Pro trial record

To give you a 72-hour trial of Pro features without an account, the app keeps a small record on the device. It contains only dates and times (such as the first launch) and a few yes/no flags (for example, whether Pro was confirmed by the store). It contains no personal data, no device identifier and no name or email.

  • iPhone and iPad: the record is kept in the iOS Keychain on your device. It is not synced through iCloud Keychain. Because it is in the Keychain, it can remain on the device after you delete and reinstall the app, so the trial is not restarted by reinstalling.
  • Android: the record is a small file in the app's private storage. If you have Android's backup to your Google account turned on, Android may include it in that backup (see "Backups" below).

"Delete everything" in Settings erases your calculator data and settings, but not the trial record.

Things that happen only when you choose them

Exactica never sends anything on its own. Some features hand information to another app only when you tap a button:

  • Share, export and backup: results, the tape, amortization tables (text, CSV or PDF) and the backup file are passed to your system share sheet. You choose where they go (for example Files, Mail or a messenger). What happens next is governed by the app you pick.
  • Restore from file: you pick a backup file with the system file picker; the app reads only that file.
  • Copy and paste: the app reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste or import a theme.
  • Report a calculation error / Contact: opens your own mail app with a draft. The report shows you its full text first: app and engine version, phone model and system version, the calculator's current state, the last tape lines and keys pressed. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.
  • Links (store page, this policy, manuals) open in your browser or store app, outside Exactica.

Purchases (Exactica Pro)

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment, billing details, purchase history and receipts are processed by Apple or Google, not by us, under their privacy policies:

  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app does not send the stores any account name or user identifier of its own. It only asks the store whether Pro is owned on this device's store account. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.

On Android, Google's Play Billing library (included in apps that sell in-app purchases) has its own diagnostic logging. Exactica's Android app has no Internet permission, so this library cannot send data from inside the app; the purchase itself is carried out by the Google Play Store app.

Backups made by your phone

Exactica's data can be included in backups your device makes (iCloud or computer backups on iOS; Android backup to your Google account). You control these in system settings; Apple or Google handle them. We have no access to them.

Permissions

Exactica does not ask for your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, calendar or notifications. On Android it declares only the Google Play purchase permission (BILLING) and two technical permissions added by Google's and Android's libraries (network state, and an internal one protecting the app's own components). It does not declare the Internet permission.

Children

Exactica is a general-audience calculator. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We hold no personal data, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove all app data with Settings → Delete everything or by deleting the app (on iOS the trial record may remain, as described above). For purchase data, contact Apple or Google.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change ships.
